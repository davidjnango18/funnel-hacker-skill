# Global Operational Profile

Use this fallback when no country-specific profile applies.

- Preserve original language, locale, market, currency, dates, and source context.
- Ask only for choices that materially affect the deliverable; otherwise proceed with neutral language and label assumptions.
- Verify market-dependent payments, claims, regulations, fulfillment, taxes, guarantees, and platform rules at research time.
- Avoid treating one market's evidence as universally transferable.
- Separate semantic translation, persuasive principle, and localized execution.
- Record locale variants as separate nodes when funnels differ by country or language.
