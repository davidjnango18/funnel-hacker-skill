# es-MX Operational Profile

Use this as a checklist, not a collection of cultural truths.

- Preserve source-market evidence separately from Mexican adaptation.
- Use natural Mexican Spanish appropriate to the verified audience and brand; avoid unexplained regionalisms from other markets.
- Verify currency (`MXN`, `$`, or explicit `MX$`) and amount formatting in the actual context.
- Verify date/time format, timezone, delivery geography, taxes, and current payment/installment language when material.
- Confirm product availability, guarantees, claims, testimonials, and legal/compliance applicability in Mexico before reusing them.
- Review CTA/form language, `tú`/`usted`, WhatsApp terminology, and support expectations against observed brand usage or user guidance.
- Keep Portuguese/English source copy and the persuasive principle visible during adaptation.

If a market-dependent fact is not verified, mark it `MISSING` or `INFERRED`; do not convert it into a localization rule.
