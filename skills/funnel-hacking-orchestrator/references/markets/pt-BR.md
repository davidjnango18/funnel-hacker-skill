# pt-BR Operational Profile

Use this as a checklist, not a collection of cultural truths.

- Preserve Brazilian Portuguese evidence in the original before translating or abstracting it.
- Use natural pt-BR appropriate to the verified audience and brand; do not normalize it into European Portuguese.
- Verify currency (`BRL`, `R$`) and amount formatting in the actual context.
- Verify date/time format, timezone, delivery geography, taxes, and current payment/installment terminology when material.
- Confirm product availability, guarantees, claims, testimonials, and legal/compliance applicability in Brazil before reusing them.
- Review CTA/form language, WhatsApp terminology, and register against observed brand usage or user guidance.
- When adapting to Mexico, extract the persuasive principle first; a literal pt-BR to es-MX translation is not localization.

If a market-dependent fact is not verified, mark it `MISSING` or `INFERRED`; do not convert it into a localization rule.
