# Market Localization

## Required Separation

Use this chain:

`ORIGINAL -> understand message -> extract persuasive principle -> translate meaning -> adapt to verified market context -> review final copy`

Keep these artifacts distinct:

1. source-language excerpt or summary with Source ID;
2. literal/semantic translation when useful;
3. persuasive principle;
4. localized strategic adaptation;
5. original final copy.

A translated funnel is not automatically localized.

## Localization Checklist

- language and regional register;
- currency and amount formatting;
- date/time formatting and timezone;
- verified payment methods and installment terminology;
- examples, references, units, and vocabulary;
- brand voice and platform conventions;
- legal, policy, or claim constraints when relevant;
- CTA clarity and form-field expectations;
- evidence/proof portability across markets.

Do not assume a payment method, cultural preference, legal constraint, or buying behavior. Verify current facts during the investigation when they matter.

## Evidence Portability

Ask whether a claim or proof asset validly transfers across market, language, product formulation, price, date, and audience. If not, keep it as source-market evidence and mark the localization need.

## Review Passes

1. semantic accuracy;
2. terminology/glossary consistency;
3. persuasive function preserved;
4. market facts verified;
5. natural regional language;
6. claims/proof traced;
7. no competitor expression copied.
