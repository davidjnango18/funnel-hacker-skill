# Launches and PLF/CPL Sequences

## Scope

Primary sequence source: Walker (`SRC-007`). Supporting sources: Brunson (`SRC-006`), Cialdini (`SRC-003`), Schwartz (`SRC-002`), CXL (`SRC-010`, `SRC-011`), and Hormozi Offers (`SRC-008`).

## Sequence map

`PRE-PRELAUNCH → PLC 1 (WHY) → PLC 2 (WHAT) → PLC 3 (HOW/OWNERSHIP) → OPEN → CLOSE → FULFILL/LEARN`

This is a classification and planning model, not a mandatory number of videos or days.

## Phase jobs

| Phase | Primary job | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-prelaunch | activate attention; collect language, questions, and demand signals | survey/reply prompts, timestamps, response context |
| PLC 1 | establish opportunity and relevance | problem/outcome language, positioning, initial objections |
| PLC 2 | teach and make transformation plausible | demonstration, proof, questions, consumption |
| PLC 3 | show implementation/ownership and bridge to offer | fit, objections, offer disclosure, real constraint |
| Open | explain offer, terms, proof, and action | price, guarantee, bonuses, availability, CTA |
| Close | resolve remaining uncertainty and enforce only real deadline | stated deadline basis, reminder cadence, final terms |
| Post-launch | fulfill and capture learning | support, refunds, activation, buyer/non-buyer feedback |

## Investigation protocol

1. Reconstruct actual chronology from timestamps and captured assets.
2. Mark missing email, video, community, or WhatsApp history only when detected.
3. Tag each asset by phase/job without assuming it follows PLF.
4. Trace repeated claims, proof, objections, and changes across the sequence.
5. Record urgency/scarcity as an observed statement; verify its basis when possible.
6. Separate author/competitor case claims from owned outcomes.

## Cross-source constraints

- Cialdini can explain plausible influence cues, but does not prove a launch caused observed results.
- Schwartz helps adapt the entry point by awareness; not every list needs problem education.
- CXL turns launch feedback into research evidence and test hypotheses.
- Hormozi helps inspect offer value and risk; it does not validate the launch cadence.
- Current email/platform rules and testimonial requirements must be checked live.

## Testable tensions

- concentrated launch event versus evergreen availability;
- three-part education versus shorter or interactive sequences;
- broad list reach versus best-fit segmentation;
- real deadline urgency versus open enrollment;
- high content volume versus consumption and fatigue.

Choose based on product, audience, operational constraint, evidence, and owned measurement—not practitioner authority.
