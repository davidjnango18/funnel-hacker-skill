## Why this source matters

Hormozi separates contact records from **engaged leads**—people who show an observable interest—and organizes acquisition around four primary actions plus four ways to enlist others. The value is a channel and lead-magnet planning framework, not a promise of volume or economics.

## Core concepts

- **Engaged lead:** a prospect who takes an observable interest action; a database row alone is not equivalent.
- **Offer and lead magnet:** acquisition depends on what is offered for attention/contact, not only media execution.
- **Core Four:** warm outreach, free content, cold outreach, and paid ads.
- **Lead getters:** customers/referrals, employees, agencies, and affiliates/partners can execute or amplify the Core Four.
- **More, better, new:** increase volume, improve execution, or add channels/audiences in a controlled order.
- **Give value before purchase:** demonstrate competence and reduce uncertainty, while ensuring the free solution connects naturally to the paid job.

## Frameworks

### Core Four matrix

| Relationship | One-to-one | One-to-many |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known/earned access | Warm outreach | Publish free content |
| Unknown/paid or interruptive access | Cold outreach | Paid ads |

The matrix is a planning abstraction. Consent, platform rules, deliverability, costs, and market norms determine whether a tactic is appropriate.

### Seven-step lead-magnet design

1. Choose a narrow, meaningful problem and a specific audience.
2. Choose a solution type: reveal/diagnose a problem, provide a sample/trial, or deliver one step of a larger process.
3. Choose a delivery mode: software/tool, information, service, or physical product.
4. Test truthful packaging—name, headline, image, subheadline.
5. Reduce consumption effort.
6. Deliver genuine standalone value.
7. Make the next request clear and proportionate.

### Problem-solution continuity

A useful lead magnet resolves a narrow problem and reveals a legitimate next job that the core offer can solve. It should not manufacture anxiety, withhold a necessary conclusion, or disguise the paid requirement.

## Diagnostic signals

- lead volume reported without a definition of engagement or qualification;
- magnets unrelated to the paid offer;
- content that attracts a broad audience but no plausible buyers;
- polls or reactions presented as purchase intent;
- channel expansion before message/offer quality is diagnosed;
- hidden fulfillment cost, low consumption, weak handoff, or poor downstream quality;
- referral/affiliate arrangements without disclosure or incentive clarity.

## Funnel and creative applications

- Record the exact engagement event: reply, opt-in, booked call, trial activation, qualified application, or another defined action.
- Map each competitor lead magnet to problem, delivery mode, next problem, and CTA.
- Test distinct problem/solution hypotheses, not only titles.
- Segment downstream quality by source and magnet using owned data.
- Treat observed creative longevity as duration only, never proof of profitable lead generation.

## Questions this source helps answer

- What turns exposure into observable interest?
- Which acquisition mode is in use?
- Does the lead magnet solve a meaningful narrow problem?
- Does free value create a coherent and ethical transition to the core offer?
- Should the next effort be more, better, or new?

## Misuse risks and limitations

- The framework is practitioner guidance; anecdotes and author-reported results are not causal evidence.
- “Engaged” does not mean qualified, incremental, or profitable.
- Aggressive outreach can violate consent, deliverability, platform, privacy, or local-law requirements.
- More channels can multiply noise and operational burden.
- Testing names with a small poll yields directional feedback, not validated demand.
- Current costs, formats, targeting, and platform rules must be checked live.

## Key operational takeaways

- Define engagement and qualification before counting leads.
- Design the magnet around a narrow job and coherent next job.
- Diagnose offer/message quality before adding channel volume.
- Measure downstream value, not just opt-ins.
