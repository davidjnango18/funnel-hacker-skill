# Creative Strategy, Hooks, and Angles

## Scope

Synthesis of Hopkins (`SRC-001`), Schwartz (`SRC-002`), Sharp (`SRC-004`), Dunford (`SRC-005`), Hormozi Leads (`SRC-009`), and CXL (`SRC-010`, `SRC-011`).

## Strategic unit

An **angle** is a coherent hypothesis about audience state, problem/desire, comparative value, mechanism, and proof. A **hook** is the entry expression that earns attention for that angle. Changing only wording or format does not necessarily create a new angle.

## Creative hypothesis record

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Audience/occasion | who may care and in what buying situation |
| Awareness | current problem/solution/product knowledge |
| Sophistication | category claim/mechanism saturation |
| Competitive alternative | what the prospect would otherwise do |
| Angle | problem, outcome, comparison, identity, proof, or mechanism frame |
| Hook | first attention/meaning unit |
| Proof | evidence available for material assertions |
| Brand code | distinctive asset that supports attribution |
| Destination continuity | how the landing experience fulfills the setup |
| Metric/guardrail | owned measure and downstream risk |

## Diagnostic signals

- many “variants” share the same strategic premise;
- the hook creates curiosity but selects the wrong audience;
- a saturated promise is enlarged without new proof;
- mechanism language is novel but non-explanatory;
- creative wins attention while obscuring the brand;
- the destination changes the promise or comparison frame;
- an ad's age or library presence is called performance evidence.

## Cross-source synthesis

- Hopkins: select the right prospect and make a specific, accountable proposition.
- Schwartz: vary entry by awareness and sophistication.
- Dunford: anchor value against real alternatives.
- Sharp: preserve recognizable brand assets and do not over-narrow reach without evidence.
- Hormozi: clarify the free/paid value exchange and observable engagement action.
- CXL: use research and owned tests to decide, not taste or competitor imitation.

## Creative testing order

Prefer testing materially different angles before minor execution details when uncertainty is strategic. Then refine hook, proof, format, visual, CTA, and landing continuity. The exact order depends on evidence and production cost; it is not a universal rule.
